# Design System: Garb by GTC

## 1. Visual Theme & Philosophy
Garb by GTC's design language reflects modern Indian haute couture and commercial ethnic wear heritage. Built on an organic creamish-ivory ground accented by rich espresso brown and warm camel sand, it evokes tactile textile luxury, architectural restraint, and commercial confidence.

## 2. Color Palette
- **Ivory Ground (`#FAF7F2`)**: Warm, non-reflective silk surface providing effortless breathing room.
- **Ivory Card Container (`#F4EFE6`)**: Tactile separation for cards and modal sheets without harsh borders.
- **Brown Primary Ink (`#2D2119`)**: High-contrast, deep warm espresso ensuring WCAG AAA accessibility.
- **Sand Accent (`#A68D74`)**: Delicate metallic thread tones echoing zari and zardozi detailing.

## 3. Typography
- **Display Headings (`Marcellus`)**: Roman proportions, sculptural serifs, and quiet dignity suited for luxury ethnic wear.
- **Interface & Body (`Manrope`)**: High legibility, neutral geometric clarity, and crisp tabular numbers for commercial B2B forms.

## 4. Layout & Craft Rules
- **No Side-Tab Borders**: Cards rely on unified elevation, soft border definition (`border border-ivory-300`), and subtle shadows.
- **No Eyebrow Kickers**: Headings command authority on their own.
- **Real Imagery**: Genuine product samples, founder portrait, and team studio photography.
- **Direct B2B Actionability**: Frictionless WhatsApp integration and instant retail partner onboarding.
