## Platform
web

## Stack
Vite + React + Tailwind CSS + Lucide Icons + Framer Motion

## Users
- Multi-brand outlet (MBO) owners, retail store buyers, boutique curators, wholesale apparel agents, and retail partners across India seeking high-demand, commercial-rate ethnic wear.
- End-consumer women looking for stylish, distinctive, quality ethnic apparel ("Just Be You").

## Product Purpose
Deliver a high-conversion, modern, sleek, minimal landing and brand experience for Garb by GTC. Establish the 20-year brand legacy (founded in 2004 by Mr. Gopal Boria, serving 500+ MBOs across India), showcase the diverse ethnic collections (kurtis, tunics, cord sets, 3-piece sets, gowns, contemporary ethnic wear), highlight retail value props (hard-to-find designs, commercial rates, trusted quality), and drive wholesale retail partner inquiries directly through a dedicated B2B form and WhatsApp connect.

## Positioning
Ethnic wear that unites hard-to-find distinctive designs, reliable craftsmanship, and genuine commercial pricing tailored for 500+ MBO partners across India — bridging boutique-level exclusivity with retail-scale profitability.

## Operating Context
Multi-brand retail buyers and fashion entrepreneurs evaluating collections on mobile devices and desktops, looking for catalog variety, fabric quality assurance, showroom/factory location in Dadar West Mumbai, and fast agent/retail onboarding.

- Showroom location and contact card: Shop No. 15, Ground Floor, Jay Gopal Industrial Estate, B.S. Cross Road, Dadar West, Mumbai, Maharashtra with one-click Google Maps navigation & phone dialer (+91 85915 61746).
