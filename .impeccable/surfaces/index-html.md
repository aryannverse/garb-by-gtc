# Surface Brief: Garb by GTC Landing

## Scope
Web landing page (`index.html`)

## Visitor Mode
Persuade

## Audience & Job
- Audience: Multi-brand outlet (MBO) retailers, boutique owners, apparel agents across India, and modern women seeking distinctive ethnic wear.
- Job: Discover Garb by GTC's collection (kurtis, tunics, cord sets, 3-piece sets, gowns), verify their 20-year manufacturing trust (founded 2004 by Mr. Gopal Boria, 500+ MBOs), and partner directly via the wholesale inquiry form or WhatsApp.

## Direction contract

THESIS: An ultra-sleek, minimalist atelier showcase for Indian ethnic wear that rejects generic noisy ecommerce clutter. Unites heritage craftsmanship with contemporary editorial typography, tactile cream-ivory grounds, deep espresso brown accents, and fluid micro-interactions.

OWN-WORLD: Pinned palette of warm creamish-ivory ground (#FAF7F2, #F4EFE6), rich deep espresso brown (#281E18, #3D2E24), and soft warm camel/sand accents (#C4A482, #E8DFD3). Sculptural typography, smooth button hovers, silky category filters, and curated whitespace.

STORY: The visitor grasps Garb by GTC's 20-year stature (founded 2004 by Mr. Gopal Boria, serving 500+ MBOs across India). They explore the diverse collections through an interactive gallery with category tabs and modal zoom, learn the 3 core advantages (hard-to-find designs, commercial rates, trusted quality), see the founder and team, find the Dadar West showroom, and submit wholesale inquiries.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Architectural header with brand logo, direct call link, and WhatsApp button. Bold yet refined headline "Garb by GTC – India’s Leading Fashion Brand" with philosophical subline "Confidence. Comfort. Style. Because every woman deserves to express herself in her own way." Dual CTA ("Explore Collections" & "Become a Retail Partner"), flanked by high-impact editorial imagery from the actual product catalog.
